BEAUVILLE SEZ · LOA ISLAND · HALELOA

A speculative experiment in redefining sovereignty, capital, and people — searching for its coordinates in the Pacific.

Beauville SEZ — a speculative novel about sovereignty, capital, and migration. Follow a proposal born in a G7 summit room as an idealistic blueprint gets negotiated into reality, one deal at a time — and slowly reveals the cracks running through it.

We invite participants from around the world to help shape a new social contract — redefining public value, the right to migrate, and collective prosperity for the age of the Beau Dollar.

CIVIC REFLECTIONS

The questions Beauville is asking. What's your answer?

  • When both "supporting them" and "deporting them" are dead ends, what's the third way?
  • If every child is an immigrant, does "us versus them" still exist?
  • A zone that welcomes every faith but caps the size of every place of worship — does that protect belief, or tame it?
  • Could a special zone built to house undocumented migrants end up surpassing the very Western cities that created it?

Find the answers in the first six chapters of the novel. Explore Beauville to start reading.
