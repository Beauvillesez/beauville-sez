AN INVITATION

Share Your Story

This isn't an application form, and you don't need to play a role.

We want to hear your story — an experience, a turning point, a decision you still think about. It can be about leaving, about starting over, about anything you feel is worth remembering. It doesn't need to be complete or polished. Write as much or as little as you like.

If you're not sure where to start, think about this: has there ever been something you left behind and never went back to?

If your story moves us, it may be woven into Beauville's world — becoming part of a character, or the seed of a plot. Your name won't appear, and details will be reshuffled and altered so no one could recognize you.

Only if you'd like us to follow up.
