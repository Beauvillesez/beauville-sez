THE NOVEL

Beauville: Built by Migrants

A speculative novel about sovereignty, capital, and migration. Follow a proposal born in a G7 summit room as an idealistic blueprint gets negotiated into reality, one deal at a time — and slowly reveals the cracks running through it. Chapters are published serially on Medium.

1.1

The Third Option

The premise: what if the world's migration crisis could be solved not by walls or welfare, but by building something entirely new in the South Pacific?

 1.2

Two Approaches, Both Failing

Marcus just told a room full of world leaders that "illegal migrants" is the wrong word entirely. Now he has to prove it.

 1.3

The Numbers, and the Man Who Stopped Listening

Marcus lays out exactly how the money works — until one man at the table makes it clear he was never really listening to any of it.

 1.4

History Has Already Rendered Its Verdict

The French President asks who's left holding the bag if this doesn't work. Marcus answers with hard collateral, sovereign guarantees, and a historical comparison that turns the whole room quiet.

 1.5

Religion and Public Order

Marcus draws a hard line on religion in the public square, and two European leaders push back immediately. Then he makes a prediction about what this zone becomes in twenty years that leaves the room in dead silence.

 1.6

Finding the Right Island

Marcus narrows five Pacific islands down to one — Haleloa. Then France turns to Australia and asks the question everyone's been avoiding: are you in?

New chapters are added as they're published.
