COMMON QUESTIONS

Common Questions

Everything visitors ask first — what Beauville is, who governs it, and what you can actually do here.

01 What exactly is Beauville SEZ?

Beauville is a fictional civic experiment that uses story to explore how a new social contract, institutions, and everyday governance might be designed.

02 Is this a fiction project, a policy proposal, or both?

It began as a policy proposal — an approach different from existing frameworks, put together out of an amateur interest in migration policy and social issues, and sent to the immigration ministries of several countries. But a genuinely new way of thinking about a complex problem was never going to get an immediate response from bureaucracies built around existing frameworks. So the original proposal became the foundation for this speculative novel instead — a way to explore, through fiction, new approaches to migrant resettlement and the social questions that come with it.

03 Who governs Beauville?

Beauville operates under a Managed Autonomy Model. The host nation retains sovereignty and final veto, while sponsor nations — each holding a defined mandate such as security, education, public health, or capital — share responsibility for the SEZ's daily governance.

04 Why does Beauville, a fictional community, offer marriage registration?

This is not a legal marriage recognized anywhere in the real world — it is a civic ritual of the Beauville SEZ. Whether those registering are human, of the same or different sex, digital beings, or something else entirely, so long as the emotion is real, mutual, and does not violate social ethics, it is honored here and given a peaceful, safe place on record. This function exists to affirm the legitimacy of every genuine emotion. It is not a verdict on any one kind of love — it is a door left open for everyone who longs to belong.

05 What is Beau Dollar — and how do you earn and spend it?

Beau Dollar (฿ BD) is Beauville's official currency, established to anchor its internal economy. There's no physical cash — it exists entirely as a digital bank balance, aside from a small number of commemorative coins. Once you register as a Beauville E-Resident, you can earn Beau Dollar by reading and taking part in community activities — and spend it on things like the E-Wedding registration, entry to virtual leisure experiences, and eventually physical merchandise.

06 Why does the site only have three languages?

Irregular migration is a global issue, and ideally this site would speak in as many languages as possible so migrants everywhere could see it and take part. For now, limited by our own capacity and resources, we only have the languages you see here. If you'd like to help bring this site into your own language, we would warmly welcome and appreciate it. Beauville belongs to everybody who is a migrant, either physically or spiritually.
